# Public visual sources

- `falco-architecture.webp`: FALCO-WAFER architecture, author's paper/presentation. Paper DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/ITC-Asia67627.2025.00016 . Used to illustrate the author's own publication.
- `texture-samples.webp`: Texture-AD representative textures from the author's research presentation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.06367 . Used to illustrate the author's own publication.
- `packaging-model.webp`: owner's 2.5D COMSOL research model from personal presentation.
- `robot.webp`: real FPGA competition prototype photograph from owner's presentation. Distinct from the separately linked STM32 robot-car repository.
- `falco-results.webp`: author's FALCO-WAFER result figure retained as an asset; homepage and Publications now show the complete architecture.
- `cuhk-campus.jpg`: CUHK campus photograph from https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/english/images/campus/banner-campus.jpg , source page https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/english/campus/campus.html . Institution credited beside displayed image. No university endorsement implied.
- `Shurong-Cao-CV.pdf`: newly typeset public CV, September 2026. Not a copy of the original private-contact CV.

Original research ownership and publication rights remain with their respective authors and publishers. This repository does not relicense third-party imagery or paper figures.
